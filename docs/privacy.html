SafeDiscreet has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. It does not create a profile of you, it does not track you, and it shows no advertising. Nothing you record leaves your iPhone unless you deliberately export or share it yourself.

This policy explains what that means in practice, and what the app does keep on your device.

1. Who this policy is from

SafeDiscreet is published by Muhammet Can Kilic (“we”, “us”). Questions about this policy can be sent to safediscreetrecorder@gmail.com

2. Personal data we collect: none

We do not collect, receive, store or process any personal data about you. Specifically, SafeDiscreet:

  • has no user accounts, sign-in, email capture or registration of any kind;
  • contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or attribution software;
  • contains no third-party SDKs of any kind, and has no server of ours to talk to;
  • does not read your contacts, calendar, messages, health data or photo library;
  • does not collect a device identifier, advertising identifier or IP address.

Because we hold nothing about you, there is no database for us to search, disclose, sell or lose.

SafeDiscreet does use two of Apple's own services, and only these two: Apple Maps, to draw the small map on a recording's detail screen, and Apple's geocoding service, to turn a recorded coordinate into a readable place name such as a street or district. Both are described in section 4. They are requests from your device to Apple, handled under Apple's privacy policy; we neither see nor receive anything from them.

4. Permissions, and what each one is used for

  • Camera — used only while you are recording or previewing on the recorder screen. SafeDiscreet never runs the camera in the background.
  • Microphone — used only while a video or audio recording is running.
  • Location (while using the app) — optional, and a Premium feature. When enabled, the coordinates and time of capture are written into the recording file itself, in the same standard fields the system Camera app uses. If you do not grant this permission, or you are on the free tier, no location is recorded at all.
    Two things happen with that coordinate beyond writing it into the file, and both go to Apple rather than to us: it is sent to Apple's geocoding service to obtain a readable place name, which is then stored alongside the recording; and when you open a recording that has a location, the detail screen loads map tiles for that area from Apple Maps. Nothing about your location reaches us at any point.
  • Photos (add only) — SafeDiscreet requests add-only access, which lets it place a file into your library but never lets it read your library. If you use the optional “Import from library” feature, the items you pick are handed to the app by the system picker, which runs outside the app; SafeDiscreet still cannot browse anything you did not choose.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — handled entirely by iOS. SafeDiscreet asks the system to authenticate you and receives only a yes or no. Your biometric data never leaves the Secure Enclave and is never available to the app.

Every one of these can be changed at any time in Settings ▸ SafeDiscreet. Camera and Microphone are the only two the app cannot function without.

5. Subscriptions and payments

SafeDiscreet Premium is an auto-renewable subscription sold and processed by Apple. Payment is handled by the App Store under your Apple Account. We never see your name, card details, billing address or Apple Account email; we receive only anonymous, aggregated sales reports from Apple.

The app asks Apple's on-device subscription service whether an active subscription exists, and that answer alone decides which features are available. This check does not involve any server of ours.

6. When data does leave your device

Three cases, and in none of them does anything reach us:

  • Saving to Photos. On the free tier this is where video and photos are saved. Once a file is in your Photos library, your own Photos and iCloud settings govern it.
  • Exporting or sharing. The export button hands the file to the standard iOS share sheet so you can send it to a lawyer, a colleague, a cloud drive or anywhere else. Once a file leaves SafeDiscreet, its protection is whatever the destination provides. We have no visibility into and no control over what happens to it afterwards.
  • Apple Maps and geocoding. As described in section 4, a coordinate you chose to record is sent to Apple to resolve a place name and to draw a map. This is a request from your device to Apple, under Apple's terms, and it never involves us. It happens only for recordings that have a location — which means never on the free tier, and never if you declined the location permission.

7. Children

SafeDiscreet is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children — and since we collect no information from anyone, there is nothing for us to hold or delete.

8. Your rights

Data-protection laws including the EU/UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to its processing.

These rights are directed at whoever holds your data. In SafeDiscreet's case that is only you: your recordings are on your own device, under your own control, and we hold no copy. You can exercise all of them yourself — delete an individual recording inside the vault, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. We cannot delete your data on request, because we do not have it.

If you believe this description is inaccurate, or you want to ask a question about it, write to safediscreetrecorder@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Security, honestly stated

Vault files are protected by iOS Data Protection, which ties the encryption key to your device passcode; the vault is unlocked with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, and optionally a separate app passcode you set. Each file's SHA-256 digest is recorded when it is saved, so you can later check whether that file has changed.

What this does not do: it is not a guarantee against a determined attacker with physical access to an unlocked device, it is not a third-party certification of your recording, and it cannot protect a file after you export it. No security measure is absolute, and we do not claim otherwise.

4. İzinler ve her birinin ne için kullanıldığı

  • Kamera — yalnızca kayıt ekranındayken, kayıt alırken veya önizleme sırasında kullanılır. SafeDiscreet kamerayı arka planda asla çalıştırmaz.
  • Mikrofon — yalnızca bir video veya ses kaydı sürerken kullanılır.
  • Konum (uygulama kullanılırken) — isteğe bağlıdır ve Premium özelliğidir. Etkinleştirildiğinde çekim anının koordinatları ve saati doğrudan kayıt dosyasının içine, sistem Kamera uygulamasının kullandığı standart alanlara yazılır. Bu izni vermezseniz ya da ücretsiz sürümdeyseniz hiçbir konum kaydedilmez.
    Koordinatla, dosyaya yazılmasının ötesinde iki şey daha olur ve ikisi de bize değil Apple'a gider: okunabilir bir yer adı elde etmek için Apple'ın coğrafi kodlama servisine gönderilir ve dönen yer adı kaydın yanında saklanır; konumu olan bir kaydı açtığınızda ayrıntı ekranı o bölgenin harita karolarını Apple Haritalar'dan yükler. Konumunuza dair hiçbir şey hiçbir aşamada bize ulaşmaz.
  • Fotoğraflar (yalnızca ekleme) — SafeDiscreet yalnızca ekleme izni ister; bu izin kitaplığınıza dosya koymasına izin verir, kitaplığınızı okumasına asla izin vermez. İsteğe bağlı “Kitaplıktan içe aktar” özelliğini kullanırsanız, seçtiğiniz öğeler uygulamanın dışında çalışan sistem seçicisi tarafından uygulamaya verilir; SafeDiscreet yine de sizin seçmediğiniz hiçbir şeye bakamaz.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — tamamen iOS tarafından yürütülür. SafeDiscreet sistemden sizi doğrulamasını ister ve yalnızca evet/hayır cevabını alır. Biyometrik verileriniz Secure Enclave'den hiç çıkmaz ve uygulamaya hiçbir zaman açılmaz.

Bunların hepsini istediğiniz zaman Ayarlar ▸ SafeDiscreet üzerinden değiştirebilirsiniz. Uygulamanın onlarsız çalışamadığı tek iki izin Kamera ve Mikrofon'dur.
